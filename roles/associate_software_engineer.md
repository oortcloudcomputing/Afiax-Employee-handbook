# Associate Software Engineer

Our engineers build software that makes our clients happy. They prefer problem solving over completing tasks, love learning from colleagues, and work as a unified team to help deliver projects that make a real difference to people’s lives. 

## Your role at Afiax

We primarily write and deliver custom software for the healthcare sector. Technical excellence for us isn’t about delivering to feature lists. We place a strong emphasis on outcome-based delivery, making sure our clients’ goals are understood and achieved with the technology we deploy.

Our teams have used Ruby with Rails and Sinatra, ES6 with React and Angular 2, and C# with .NET Core. We don’t limit ourselves and we expect all of our engineers to be keen on learning new technologies. Automation is important to our teams, so we make sure there’s a CD pipeline set up to build, test, and release. We’re usually responsible for setting up clients’ infrastructure, too. For example, on AWS, GCP or Azure using tools like Terraform. Though, sometimes we opt for a Platform as a Service like Heroku.

## What experience are we looking for?

All of our Software Engineers are trained first as an **Academy Trainee: Software Engineering**: five months of free training on the Oortcloud Academy and supervised project work, on a KES 25,000 monthly stipend ([how people join](../guides/compensation/pay_bands.md)).

## Salary and location

We mainly work remotely but you may need to visit clients or go to the office occasionally. We have office in Nairobi, Kenya. 

We practice salary transparency when it comes to advertising roles at Afiax. Every role we publish will include the salary range in the job ad, please do refer to that.

Associate Software Engineer: **KES 80,000 – 130,000 a month** ([all pay bands](../guides/compensation/pay_bands.md)).

## Applying

We don't hire directly for Associate Software Engineer roles at Afiax. Apply as an **Academy Trainee: Software Engineering**: after five months of training and a passed training review, you become an Associate Software Engineer, with a 3-month probation. Open positions, including the Academy, are listed at https://www.afiax.africa/career/. We’d still love to hear from you if you feel our academy or more senior engineering roles aren’t a good fit for you. If you have any questions, drop us a line at [careers@afiax.africa](mailto:careers@afiax.africa).
