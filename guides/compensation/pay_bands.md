# Pay bands, the Academy and how people join

*Adopted 17 November 2026. Every role we advertise shows its range; the ranges below are monthly gross pay in
Kenya shillings (KES), before PAYE and statutory deductions.*

## Four ways to join Oortcloud

| Track | Who | Training | Pay | Then |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Academy Trainee** | Graduates and career-changers | **5 months**, free: the Oortcloud Academy courses for your track, then supervised work on real projects, with a named mentor | **KES 25,000 a month** training stipend | Pass the training review: an **Associate** role on its band, with a 3-month probation |
| **Industrial attachment** | Students still enrolled at a university or TVET | 3 months (or your institution's period), free, with a mentor | **KES 10,000 a month** allowance | Strong students are invited to the Academy |
| **Direct hire** | Experienced people | Onboarding and the required Academy courses | The role's band from day one | [3-month probation](../line-management/probation.md) |
| **Founders and co-founders** | Owners | Founders and Leaders Onboarding | Equity, under the shareholder agreement (not a job offer) | — |

The Academy itself is free: nobody pays to train with us. Trainees and attachés are paid as shown. Anyone on
probation is an employee and paid at least the role's band: Kenya's Employment Act 2007 limits probation to six
months (extendable by agreement to twelve), and the minimum wage applies (KES 18,047.40 a month in Nairobi from
May 2026).

## Where roles are advertised

Open positions are published from the console's HR (Frappe HR job openings) and appear on
[afiax.africa/career](https://www.afiax.africa/career/) with their pay. Applications go through the job's page.
